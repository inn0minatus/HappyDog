# **Brief — 2D Browser Runner — BI**

### **1. General Information and Objective**

- **Brand:** Veterinary segment.

- **Product:** Flea and tick medication for dogs.

- **Format:** 2D Endless Runner / Level Runner in the browser (Mobile-first Web / HTML5).

- **Project objective:** Raise awareness of protecting animals from ticks through a gaming experience, and promote the tablet form of protection.

### **2. Target Platforms and Mechanics**

- **Platform:** Web (desktop and mobile browsers, cross-browser). Priority — **Mobile Web** (iOS Safari / Android Chrome).

- **Genre:** 2D Runner (the character runs left to right or forward by inertia; the player jumps / dodges).

- **Core gameplay:**

  1.  **Character selection:** At the start, the player chooses 1 of 5 dog breeds/models.

  2.  **Game round:**

      - **Collecting tablets (bonuses):** Add points / restore health.

      - **The medication as "Armor" (optional mechanic):** Designed on the principle of the mask from *Crash Bandicoot* — picking up a tablet grants a temporary shield / active armor that completely negates damage from colliding with a tick.

      - **Obstacles (ticks):** Remove health / deduct points on collision.

### **3. Technical and Functional Requirements**

#### **A. Visual & Assets (Art and Animation)**

*Please provide separate estimates in the quote for development (Code) and creative (Design/Animation).*

- **Characters:** 5 dog skins (basic animations required: run, jump, taking damage, victory/defeat).

- **Enemies and objects:** 1 type of tick, 1 type of tablet (the brand's product), additional obstacles (e.g., bushes/puddles).

- **Background:** 1–2 layers of parallax background (park, forest, city avenue).

- **UI/UX:** Main menu, dog selection screen, in-game HUD (score, HP/lives, timer/progress), results screen (Game Over / Victory).

#### **B. Architecture and Integrations**

- **Engine:** At the developers' discretion — something simple.

- **Analytics & Pixel:** Ability to integrate Google Analytics / Meta Pixel (event tracking: "start_game", "finish_game", "click_buy").

- **CTA button:** After the round ends — calls to action (e.g., "Learn more" with a link to the client's website, or receive a promo code and go to [tabletki.ua](http://tabletki.ua) to purchase the medication).
